The attendant

Mirela Danso

Mirela Danso was born in 1968 in Constanța, on the Black Sea. Her mother kept the cloakroom at the State Theatre, and Mirela did her homework under the counter among the coats. A coat went on a hook and a numbered brass token went into a hand. She says the whole trade is two things: you do not look in the pockets, and you never lose the number.

She left in the spring of 1990 with one suitcase and took night work at the station, sorting what the cleaners brought off the trains. She stayed twenty-eight years and ended as head of the lost property counter and the left-luggage hall.

Most things, she found, are not lost. They are left. People leave what they cannot carry any further. A found thing was tagged and kept for a year and a day, and then it went to auction in lots. She could not bear the auction. On her own time she traced owners, from a bus ticket in a pocket or a name inside a hat, and returned something over four thousand things. What she could not return she sometimes bought back herself: only the things with work in them. Portfolios, sketchbooks, a tin of slides, a notebook of knitting charts.

In 2019 the station closed the lower level and put coin lockers upstairs. She retired the same month and kept a key that nobody asked for. The racks were taken out. The hooks stayed in the walls. She opened the hall in 2021, after the last train, for whoever came down the stairs.

She does not call herself a curator. A curator chooses, she says. She receives, and then she is careful.

Rules she keeps

  1. A year and a day, then it comes down.
  2. Nothing from the maker's own hand. Someone else hands it in.
  3. Only work that was written down and can be made again.
  4. One sentence on the tag. Two at most.
  5. Never explain what the maker has not.
  6. A chair in every room.
  7. One hook left empty.
  8. Open after the last train. Closed when the first one is announced.

Things she has said

Lost is an accident. Left is a decision. I mostly kept decisions.

A year and a day is long enough to come back for something. If you have not come by then, it was a gift.

The hook is not empty. It is kept.

The numbering of the tags begins at 0002.
