How this gallery was made

A Year and a Day is a work of fiction. The hall, the attendant and the makers are invented, and so are their lives. Any resemblance to a real person is a coincidence. The places, trades and traditions the makers come from are real.

The pictures are real in the way the gallery says they are. Each one is drawn by a single program, written for that picture. No photographs, scans or other images go in. A place is built from geometry and lit, or a sheet of paper is wetted and inked, or a rhythm is counted and ruled, and the picture is what the program leaves.

The gallery was made by a person and an AI model working together. The roster will change. Rooms come down after a year and a day.
