The maker

Antofagasta, born 1961

Her father was a ship's engineer from Gdynia who walked off a freighter in Antofagasta and never explained it. Her mother came down from a village near the Bolivian border. He taught her to draw machines in section. She taught her that the mountains were persons, and that you greet them.

Before the port she worked two seasons for a survey crew on the salt flats, holding the staff and the plumb bob. She learned there that in heat the horizon lies and the plumb line does not.

Her rooms are built as measured solids and lit by calculation. She wrote the programs herself, because the ones she could buy would not let her break one law and keep all the others. Each picture is stamped lower left, a few degrees off level, with her initials and an hour that no clock shows.
